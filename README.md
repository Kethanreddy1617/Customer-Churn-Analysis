# Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Dashboard
# 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn data from a telecom company to identify patterns and factors associated with customer churn. The objective is to understand why customers leave and identify high-risk customer segments to support better customer retention strategies.
The project uses the IBM Telco Customer Churn sample dataset and performs data analysis using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI.

# 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn can negatively impact business revenue and growth. The objective of this project is to analyze customer data and answer the following questions:
* How many customers have churned?
* What is the overall churn rate?
* Which contract types have higher churn?
* Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?
* Which internet services have higher churn?
* Do customer tenure and monthly charges influence churn?
* Which customer groups represent a higher churn risk?

# 📌 Key Insights

* Month-to-month contract customers showed the highest churn.
* Customers using electronic check had higher churn compared to other payment methods.
* Fiber optic customers showed significant churn.
* Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to churn.
* Senior citizens represented a higher-risk customer segment.
* Churned customers had different average monthly charges compared to active customers.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* Encourage month-to-month customers to switch to one-year or two-year contracts through discounts or loyalty benefits.
* Provide targeted retention offers to high-risk payment and service segments.
* Focus on improving the experience of customers with higher monthly charges.
* Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers during their early tenure.
* Provide personalized support and retention strategies for high-risk customer groups.
